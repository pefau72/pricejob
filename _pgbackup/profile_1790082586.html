Independent Energy Sector Consulting

Peter Fausbøll is the owner of Regenda ApS. Peter Fausbøll has more than 25 years of experience in the energy sector.

Peter Fausbøll was a Senior Commercial advisor with DONG Energy during its build up of its trading floor for gas and electricity. He was also Project Manager for an extensive, technical insurance case for DONG E&P.

Peter Fausbøll has been a civil serveant for 6 years, first 4 years with the Danish Utility Regulator dealing with EU regulation of electricity markets, then 2 years with the Danish Energy Agency dealing with offshore wind auctions.

Peter Fausbøll has worked with specifying, implementing and testing Energy Trading and Risk Management systems (ETRM) in both DONG Energy and Momentum Energy. Peter Fausbøll has also worked with energy statistics systems through out his career.

Peter Fausbøll holds a Master degree in Mathematical Business Economics from Copenhagen Business School, and a Master in Wind Energy from the Technical University of Denmark.
